Saeed Damadi PhD's Documentation!
============================================

This documentation presents a comprehensive overview of my PhD research in sparse optimization and machine learning at 
the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC). 
Under the mentorship of Professor `Jinglai Shen <https://jinglai-shen.github.io/>`_, my work focuses on advanced mathematical methods for sparse optimization. 
Prior to this, I completed a master’s degree in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at UMBC, guided by Professor `Hamed Pirsiavash <https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~hpirsiav/>`_,

I have two main research goals. First, I aim to compress neural networks to better understand how the brain works. 
Second, I am passionate about advancing quantitative trading through sophisticated deep neural networks and machine learning models
